Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Doxhund is a free, manual-first privacy tool. Its stance is simple: we collect nothing.

What we don't do

  • No accounts, no signup, no login.
  • No collection of your name, address, phone number, email, or any other personal data.
  • No server-side storage of anything you type. Doxhund has no backend and no database.
  • No analytics, no trackers, no cookies, and no third-party scripts.

What actually happens when you use it

Everything runs in your browser. When you search, your name and city are used only to build links to each data broker's own site — they are never sent to Doxhund's servers, because there aren't any. When you click a link, you leave Doxhund and go directly to that broker's site.

Your removal progress (which brokers you've checked off) is stored in your browser's localStorage, on your device only. We can't see it, and it never leaves your machine. Clear your browser data and it's gone.

Defensive only

Doxhund is for self-removal — finding and deleting your own records. It is not, and never will be, a tool to look up other people.

CCPA / CPRA

Because Doxhund collects no personal information, there is nothing for us to disclose, correct, or delete under the CCPA/CPRA. The tool itself exists to help you exercise those rights against data brokers — each broker has its own opt-out process, and Doxhund gives you the links to do it yourself.

Open source & auditable

Every line is public. Inspect it, fork it, verify it: github.com/howdy-beep/doxhund.
