Official government lookups for court cases, criminal records, offenders, and custody status in Iowa. These are public records, not data brokers, so they can't be deleted with an opt-out form.

⚠️ Removal here works differently

Data brokers let you submit a deletion request. Court and criminal records don't, because they're public by law. To remove one you generally need expungement or sealing through the Iowa courts, which is a legal process, not a click. These links are for checking what's publicly out there, not removing it.

Official Iowa lookups
Iowa Courts Online
iowacourts.state.ia.us · Iowa Judicial Branch
Free public docket of Iowa state court cases. Search by party name, case number, or attorney. Full case documents are viewed at a courthouse terminal in the county where the case was filed.
Search court docket
Iowa Sex Offender Registry
iowasexoffender.gov · Iowa Department of Public Safety
Registered sex offenders in Iowa. Search by name, city, or map. You'll be asked to accept a short user agreement before the search loads.
Search registry
Iowa DOC Offender Search
doc-search.iowa.gov · Iowa Department of Corrections
Locates people currently or previously in Iowa state prisons (not county jails). Search by name or offender number.
Search offenders
Iowa VINELink
vinelink.vineapps.com/state/IA · Iowa VINE
Custody status and criminal case information. Free, and you can register for automated notifications when a person's custody status changes.
Check custody status
What these don't cover

These four cover state-level records. County records (sheriff booking logs, county jail rosters, and county-level court dockets) are held separately by each of Iowa's 99 counties and aren't aggregated here. A full Iowa criminal-history check (name plus date of birth, all counties) costs $15 via the Iowa DCI and is done by paper form, not a website search.

These are official Iowa government sources, verified November 2026. Free and open source: github.com/howdy-beep/doxhund.
