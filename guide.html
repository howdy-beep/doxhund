Before you start

1. Use a disposable email alias

Brokers harvest the email you submit on opt-out forms to re-target you. Use a throwaway alias, not your real inbox.

2. Sites demanding photo ID

Many forms ask for a "proof of identity" image. A generated face from thispersondoesnotexist.com usually satisfies the field — they rarely inspect it.

3. Start with the top 10, wait a week

The highest-impact brokers feed many of the smaller ones. Do those first, wait about 7 days, then work the rest.
